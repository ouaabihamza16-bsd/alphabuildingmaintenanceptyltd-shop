About the company

A Yagoona company for building work and upkeep

ALPHA BUILDING & MAINTENANCE PTY LTD is an Australian proprietary company based in Yagoona NSW 2199, in the Canterbury-Bankstown area of south-west Sydney. Its focus is building work and property maintenance for homes, rental properties, strata buildings and small commercial premises.

The work covers general building and repairs, planned and reactive maintenance, make-good at the end of a lease, and smaller renovations. Each job is scoped and quoted on its own, so the work matches what the property needs.

The company is registered under ABN 24 158 034 998 and ACN 158 034 998, and is registered for GST. It was previously named Triton Group Co Pty. Ltd. and took its current name on 21 October 2026. The ABN and ACN did not change.
